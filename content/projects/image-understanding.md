## The problem

Text ads and creative tooling work better when the system knows what an image actually shows. The team needed a simple way to turn any ad image into structured information.

## What it does

You send an image URL. The service returns:

- **A description** of the image, generated by a vision-language model (Moondream2 or Kimi-VL).
- **A category**, assigned by CLIP zero-shot classification, so new categories can be added without retraining.

## How I built it

I started by evaluating candidate models on output quality and cost to run. Splitting the job helped: a vision-language model is good at describing, while CLIP is fast and predictable at choosing between a fixed set of labels.

The service runs as a FastAPI app in Docker. I also built a Streamlit demo so the ads team could try their own images and see the results before we integrated it.
