## The idea

Shoppers often lose track of what their basket will cost until they reach the till. SmartBasket is a small device that clips onto the baskets a store already owns. It reads RFID tags on products as they go in and shows a running total on an e-ink display.

Because it retrofits existing baskets, a retailer doesn't have to replace equipment to try it.
